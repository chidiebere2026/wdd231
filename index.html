About Me

Hello, I am Mary Chidiebere Asoya. Frontend web development is the practice of building the visual and interactive parts of websites that users see and engage with in their browsers. In WDD 231: Introduction to Frontend Web Development, I am learning the foundational technologies—HTML for structure, CSS for styling, and JavaScript for interactivity—that work together to create responsive, user-friendly web experiences. This course emphasizes not only how to write code, but also how to design layouts and interfaces that are accessible, visually appealing, and functional across different devices.

Throughout this course, I am developing practical skills by creating real web pages and projects that reflect modern web standards and best practices. I am gaining experience in organizing content, applying styles effectively, and adding dynamic behavior to enhance user interaction. By the end of WDD 231, I aim to have a solid understanding of frontend development principles and the confidence to build and improve websites that meet both user needs and industry expectations.
